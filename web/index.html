ESP32-SEC

Bruce firmware for the Chibi ESP32-S3

A full-featured offensive-security firmware fork, built for ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8 with an ST7789V 240x320 screen and XPT2046 touch.

Authorized testing only. Use this firmware only on systems you own or are explicitly permitted to test.
